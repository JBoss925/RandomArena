HOW TO PLAY

5/5
CALL EVERY WINNER.

Random Arena gives you five deterministic ball fights. Pick the fighter you think will survive each bout. A perfect card means predicting all five correctly.

01

READ THE MATCHUP

Hover or focus a fighter’s name to reveal its playstyle, exact abilities, and core stats. The other fighter fades so you can identify it in the arena.

02

TAKE YOUR PICK

Choose Fighter A or Fighter B, then lock your pick. Once the fight starts, the prediction cannot be changed. The same seed always produces the same matchup and outcome.

03

WATCH THE FIGHT

Fighters move on their own. Bodies, held weapons, projectiles, orbiting objects, status effects, and hazards can all decide the bout. When both fighters fall below zero together, lower overkill loses.

04

CHASE THE CARD

Advance through all five bouts and aim for 5/5. Daily mode is shared by everyone; Endless creates shareable seeded cards. The hidden Versus lab lets local players inspect specific matchups.
