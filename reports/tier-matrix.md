## Method

- Every unique pairing is run from both left and right positions.
- The runner uses the production fighter definitions, behaviors, weapon hitboxes, fixed 60 Hz step, and elastic collision solver.
- No hazards are used, isolating fighter balance.
- Set `RUNS` to increase or decrease seeds per side.
